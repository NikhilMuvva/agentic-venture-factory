# Human Approval Boundaries

## Status
Accepted

## Context

AVF ventures may eventually perform actions with financial, legal, or external communication consequences.

## Decision

Require human approval for financial actions, legal actions, and external communications in the venture template.

## Alternatives

Allow all workflow actions to execute autonomously by default.

## Consequences

The default policy constrains autonomy in high-impact areas while the framework is experimental. It also means fully autonomous operation is not claimed at this stage.
