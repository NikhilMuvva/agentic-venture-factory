# Proposed Research Questions

These are proposed research questions for AVF. They are not answered findings.

## RQ1

How much can heterogeneous model routing reduce inference cost while maintaining task quality?

## RQ2

How much human intervention is required as autonomous venture workflows mature?

## RQ3

How much does multi-agent and deterministic verification improve reliability compared with single-agent generation?

## RQ4

How effectively can common AVF infrastructure generalize across heterogeneous digital ventures?
